WHY THE GAIN IS SMALLER THAN THE 2.3x PROJECTION

Decoder ITL falls 1.55 -> 1.28 ms (script), not to ~0.7. Three reasons:
  1. The local transformer is still serial per item and is 35% of a step.
     Batching it is the remaining lever (projected ~5.6x on the decoder).
  2. A wave runs until its slowest chunk finishes; finished items keep
     stepping and their output is discarded. Occupancy is mean/max over
     the group, and sentence lengths vary a lot.
  3. The codec floor (RTFx ~70 sequential) binds sooner once the decoder
     speeds up. Wave codec RTFx reads 97 because frames now arrive in
     bursts, which is a measurement artefact of batching, not a codec
     improvement.

ROOT CAUSE OF THE EARLIER WRONG AUDIO (fixed)

The local transformer composes its 8-codebook chain into one reusable
CUDA graph, and that graph captures the *device pointers* of the hidden
tensors it was composed against. Each wave item had its own
cond_hidden/uncond_hidden, so from the second chunk on the replayed
graph read the first chunk's stale hidden state: the LT emitted
near-constant codes, the decoder stalled, alignment froze, and every
chunk ran to max steps producing ~2x the audio.

Signature: chunk 0 correct, all later chunks wrong; identical at width 1;
independent of the history setting; and alignment matching the sequential
path exactly for 11 steps before freezing. The composition message prints
during chunk 0, which is what pinned it down.

Fix: one stable pair of [n_embd,1] tensors for the whole run, with each
item's hidden copied in before its sampler call. Our fork never hit this
because it allocates one wave_cond/wave_uncond pair and passes them to a
batched sampler.
